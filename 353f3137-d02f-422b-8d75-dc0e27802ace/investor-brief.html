01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

An athlete deserves more than a training plan.

Plan My Peak (PMP) is a coaching platform that turns an athlete's completed training into analysis, monitoring and feedback. It connects the planned session with the activity data that comes back, interprets the result against the athlete's recent training, flags what needs attention and delivers feedback through the coach's own service. The company is starting in cycling and sells to coaches and coaching businesses, who bring established athlete relationships and introduce PMP to their rosters.

The selling point

Athletes and coaches get more

For coaches

Scale the business without losing the human connection. PMP does the repeated analysis and monitoring, so coaches keep their judgement and relationships for the moments that matter, support more athletes, and raise the quality and consistency of the service every athlete receives. At US$200-500 a month for custom coaching, adding one athlete to the roster pays for PMP across the whole roster.

For athletes

Feedback on every session, not just when they ask: what the session showed, how it fits recent training and whether anything needs attention, with weekly and period views of progress. Athletes on an off-the-shelf plan get support that today usually ends at the schedule; athletes with a coach get a coach who sees issues earlier.

The problem

A training plan says what to do; athletes also need help understanding what happened and when to adjust. Reviewing every activity by hand limits how many athletes a coach can support, and off-the-shelf plans usually come with no follow-up at all. A 2023 study of 839 endurance coaches found planning and prescribing training took a third of their time.

The product

One analysis engine with two delivery modes. For custom coaching, PMP prepares activity, weekly and period reports that the coach reviews, edits and approves, with roster-level alerts pointing to athletes who need attention. For off-the-shelf plans, the same reports go to the athlete automatically, tuned to the coach's methodology, plus a weekly recap and an end-of-plan report.

Why now, and the path ahead

AI makes repeated analysis and feedback economical, while coaches provide the expertise and the trusted relationship through which athletes adopt it. PMP integrates with TrainingPeaks and Intervals.icu today. Triathlon, running and swimming come next, then coach-configured AI coaches and, later, services sold directly to athletes.
